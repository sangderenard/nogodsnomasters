# Licensing

## Scope

The MIT grant in [LICENSE](LICENSE) applies to the first-party code and
documentation for which sangderenard holds licensing rights. Existing
copyright and permission notices must be retained. Third-party software,
submodules, dependencies, assets and independently authored material retain
their own terms. This grant does not claim ownership of those works or
supply missing permission for unverified material.

The owner selected this first-party MIT policy on 2026-11-11 to express the
projects' continuity with SpeakToMe. Repository history and author names were
checked as provenance evidence; they are not proof of ownership of every
imported file. This document is a scoped inventory, not a complete dependency
audit or a statement that every possible combined distribution is compatible.

## Policy transition

This MIT grant replaces the inspection-only source-available notice introduced
in commit `4b22094`, for material the owner can license. The previous wording
remains in Git history. Third-party terms are not replaced.

The root repository owns its tracked coordination documents and deliberate
tracked projects such as `engine_toy/`, `site/` and `string_core/`.
Neighboring checkouts, including SpeakToMe, Turing, Nodus and spectral-analyzer,
are separate repositories with their own LICENSE and LICENSING.md files.
A parent directory or cross-repository import does not replace those notices.

## Provenance and release boundary

- The reviewed history attributes first-party commits to Albert, Albert
  Stichka and sangderenard. This does not establish rights to every embedded
  asset or generated bundle.
- Published site bundles may incorporate components produced by sibling
  repositories. Preserve the source repositories' notices and record their
  revisions with the published bundle.
- No complete inventory of external fonts, models, media, transitive
  dependencies or generated bundles is claimed by this change.
