This is Shoal, the repository’s compiled depth-averaged free-surface Navier–Stokes model. Cyan is raised water, violet is depressed water, amber is transported tracer, and brightness is flow speed. Every tile observes the same toroidal field at the same completed step; color is a measurement of its four evolving state planes, not an independent animation.
